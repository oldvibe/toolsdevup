Affiliate Disclosure

ToolsDevUp believes in being transparent about how the website may generate revenue.

What are affiliate links?

An affiliate link is a special link that allows a company to identify when a visitor was referred by ToolsDevUp.

If a visitor uses an affiliate link and completes an eligible purchase or subscription, ToolsDevUp may receive a commission.

Does this cost you more?

In general, using an affiliate link does not increase the price paid by the visitor. The company provides the commission from its own affiliate program.

Our approach

We aim to recommend tools based on their usefulness and relevance rather than solely on whether they offer an affiliate program.

We may receive compensation from some companies, but this does not mean that every tool listed on ToolsDevUp is an affiliate partner.

Current status

ToolsDevUp may contain links to official websites that are not affiliate links. Affiliate tracking links will only be used when ToolsDevUp participates in the relevant affiliate program.

Questions

If you have questions about a particular link or tool, please check the information displayed on the relevant tool page.
