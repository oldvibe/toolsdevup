Overview

ToolsDevUp respects your privacy. This page explains the general approach we currently take regarding information collected through this website.

Information we collect

ToolsDevUp currently does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to browse the tool directory.

If we introduce features that collect personal information in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Search and website interactions

The website may process information necessary for normal website functionality, such as browser requests and basic technical information.

Third-party websites

ToolsDevUp links to third-party websites and services.

When you leave ToolsDevUp and visit another website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links may eventually use affiliate tracking.

Affiliate tracking allows a partner company to recognize that a visitor came from ToolsDevUp.

Cookies

ToolsDevUp does not currently intentionally require visitors to create accounts or accept a custom tracking system.

Third-party services linked from this website may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as ToolsDevUp adds new functionality, analytics, advertising or other services.

The latest version will be published on this page.
