What is Canva?

Canva is a visual design platform designed to make creating professional looking content accessible to people without advanced graphic-design skills.

You can start with a template or create a design from scratch, then customize text, images, graphics, layouts and other elements.

What can you create?

  • University presentations
  • Posters and flyers
  • Social media graphics
  • Resumes and CVs
  • Documents
  • Videos
  • Websites and visual content

Canva Free

Canva states that Canva Free is available for individuals at no cost. Users can create designs and access a library of free templates and content.

Premium features and content are available through paid plans such as Canva Pro. Canva's current Pro offering adds features including premium content, Brand Kits and additional AI-powered capabilities.

Why students should consider Canva

🎓 Presentations

Create polished presentations for university projects, classes and presentations.

📄 CVs

Start from professional resume templates and customize them for internship applications.

📱 Social content

Quickly create graphics for personal projects, organizations and social media.

Pros

  • Easy to learn
  • Free plan available
  • Large library of templates and free design elements
  • Excellent for presentations
  • Useful for CV and portfolio design
  • Works well for non-designers

Things to consider

  • Some templates and content are premium
  • Some advanced AI and productivity features require a paid plan
  • Free users may encounter watermarks when using Pro content

Our take

Canva is one of the easiest tools to recommend to students because it can solve several problems with one platform.

Whether you need a presentation, CV, poster, social graphic or visual project, the free version provides a strong starting point.

For students who don't have a design background, Canva is particularly useful because templates handle much of the initial layout work.
