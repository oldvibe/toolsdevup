What is ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs is an AI audio platform focused on generating and working with speech, voices and other forms of audio.

It can be useful for creators, developers, students, educators and anyone who needs AI-generated audio.

What can you use it for?

  • Text-to-speech
  • Voice generation and design
  • Audio and sound creation
  • Dubbing and localization
  • AI voice features in applications

Is there a free plan?

Yes. ElevenLabs provides a free plan that allows users to try its AI audio capabilities before moving to a paid subscription.

Who is it best for?

🎓 Students

Useful for narration, presentations and creative learning projects.

💻 Developers

Useful for applications that need AI-generated voice or speech.

🎬 Creators

Useful for voiceovers, videos, podcasts and audio content.

Pros

  • Free plan available
  • Wide range of AI audio capabilities
  • Useful for creators and developers

Things to consider

  • Free usage has limits
  • Advanced capabilities may require a paid plan
  • Costs depend on usage and plan

Our take

ElevenLabs is worth exploring if you're interested in AI voice, narration or audio generation. The availability of a free plan makes it easy to experiment before deciding whether you need more capacity.
