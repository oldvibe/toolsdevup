What is Descript?

Descript is a video and audio editing platform built around transcripts. Instead of working only with a traditional timeline, you can edit spoken content by editing its text.

This makes Descript particularly useful for podcasts, interviews, tutorials, YouTube videos and other speech-heavy content.

What can you do with Descript?

  • Edit videos
  • Edit podcasts and audio
  • Generate and edit transcripts
  • Remove unwanted parts of recordings
  • Create captions and subtitles
  • Improve recorded content with AI-assisted tools

Free plan

Descript currently offers a Free plan, allowing new users to try the platform without paying for a subscription.

The current Free plan includes limited media hours and AI credits, making it suitable for testing the editing workflow before deciding whether you need a paid plan.

Who should use Descript?

🎓 Students

Useful for presentations, interviews, tutorials and project videos.

🎥 YouTubers

Edit spoken content and create captions without relying entirely on a traditional video editor.

🎙️ Podcasters

Work with transcripts and audio while keeping the editing process closely connected to the spoken content.

Pros

  • Text-based editing workflow
  • Useful AI-assisted features
  • Good fit for spoken-content creators
  • Free plan available
  • Useful for both video and audio

Things to consider

  • Free usage is limited
  • Advanced capabilities may require a paid plan
  • AI-generated transcripts and edits should be reviewed

Our take

Descript is especially interesting if most of your content involves people speaking.

Its transcript-based approach can make certain editing tasks much faster than manually searching through a traditional timeline.

For students building tutorials, project demonstrations or presentations, the free plan is a good starting point for testing the workflow.
