What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI-powered creation platform designed to help users create presentations, documents, websites, social content and images.

Instead of designing everything manually, you can describe what you want and let Gamma generate a structured starting point that you can then edit.

What can you create?

  • AI presentations
  • Documents
  • Simple websites
  • Social media content
  • AI-generated images

What does the free plan include?

Gamma currently offers a Free plan that requires no credit card to start. It includes 400 credits at signup and allows users to generate up to 10 slides from a single prompt.

Free users can also create presentations, documents, websites, social content and images, import PDF and PPTX files, and export content to PDF, PPTX, PNG and Google Slides.

Who should use Gamma?

🎓 Students

Quickly create presentations for classes, projects and university work.

💼 Professionals

Turn ideas and documents into polished presentations and visual material.

🚀 Founders

Create pitch decks, documents and simple websites faster.

Pros

  • Easy starting point for presentations
  • Free plan available
  • Supports multiple content formats
  • Can import existing PDF and PPTX files
  • Can export to common presentation formats

Things to consider

  • Free AI credits are limited
  • Advanced features are available on paid plans
  • AI-generated content should still be reviewed and edited

Our take

Gamma is particularly interesting for students because it removes much of the friction involved in creating presentations from scratch.

If you regularly make presentations, project documentation or visual material, the free plan is a useful way to test whether Gamma fits your workflow before paying for additional capabilities.
